Live Model Figure Sculpture Class
with Deane Nettles

$299 for class

Model fee included
Limit 8 students

Deane Nettles is a Baltimore-based sculptor who has been working in clay for 20 years and is co-founder of the Baltimore Sculpture Studio.

In this class, students will learn the basics of figure sculpture, creating a smaller-than-life sculpture using profiles, silhouettes, and measurements.

Students will have the opportunity to practice sculpting from a live model, focusing on capturing the proportions and essence of the figure in clay.

The class will be held on Sunday mornings from 10am‑1pm in our Station North studio at 405 East Oliver Street, Studio 354, Baltimore.

Join Our Community!

We are a collective of community-oriented, dedicated figure sculptors. We’ve created a space where we can sculpt in clay from live models in Baltimore’s Station North art district in a wonderful, light-filled studio with heating and air conditioning.

We have figure sculpture sessions with live models on Sunday afternoons.

We are looking for sculptors to join us as yearly, half-yearly and quarterly members. See membership benefits below.

We take part in open studio tours and there is a gallery in the building so there may be options to participate in gallery shows.

Our vision is to branch out with:

  • Workshops with local artists
  • Workshops with visiting artists
  • Artist talks

Sunday Studio Sessions

We have sculpture sessions with live models every Sunday from 2‑5pm.

Drop-in price: $45, including model fee and use of a stand. Must commit to the full model run, typically 8 sessions. Spots are limited; payment secures your spot.

You will need to bring your own tools and sculpting materials.

To sign up:

  • Send $45 per session via Zelle.
  • Send an email to BaltimoreSculptureStudio@gmail.com with name, phone, dates you plan to attend and copy of Zelle receipt.
