Baltimore Sculpture Studio

We are a collective of community-oriented, dedicated sculptors. We’ve created a space where we can sculpt in clay from live models in Baltimore’s Station North art district in a wonderful, light-filled studio with heating and air conditioning.

We have sculpture sessions with live models Sunday from 1‑4pm.

We are looking for sculptors to join us as yearly, half-yearly and quarterly members. See membership benefits below.

We take part in open studio tours and there is a gallery in the building so there may be options to participate in gallery shows.

Our vision is to branch out with:

  • Workshops with local artists
  • Workshops with visiting artists
  • Artist talks

Sunday Studio Sessions

We have sculpture sessions with live models Sunday from 1‑4pm.

Drop-in price: $45, including model fee and use of a stand. Spots are limited; payment secures your spot.

You will need to bring your own tools and materials.

To sign up:

  • Send $45 per session via Zelle.
  • Send an email to BaltimoreSculptureStudio@gmail.com with name, phone, dates you plan to attend and copy of Zelle receipt.

Try scanning this QR code with your banking app. To make sure you're not being defrauded, make sure Zelle says it's sending to Baltimore Sculpture Studio LLC. Or send your Zelle payment to baltimoresculpturestudio@gmail.com

If you only sign up for one session, you will need to take your materials and pieces home with you; if you sign up for several sequential sessions, we can provide storage between sessions.
